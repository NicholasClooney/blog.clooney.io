This is where AI really shines.

Today, from a conversation on my phone, an agent fleet mocked up more than eleven design directions for this blog in an afternoon: Terminal, Brutalist, Warm Paper, Swiss, Soft Glass, a retro web zine, Liquid Glass in dark and light, and three takes on sci-fi. I'd say what I liked and what felt weak, and a few minutes later there were new options to look at.

On my own, I would never have even imagined doing eleven themes. One, maybe two, over a few weekends. Now I love all eleven, and they're all going forward.

So that's the plan: these become the v2 themes, readers get to pick the one they like, and they go back into [11ty Subspace Builder](https://github.com/TheClooneyCollection/11ty-subspace-builder) so every site built on it can use them too. The current looks become v1, and they'll be sunset.
