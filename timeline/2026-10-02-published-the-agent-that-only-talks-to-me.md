I published [Messy Chat with Your Agents? You Just Need One More. Really.](/posts/the-agent-that-only-talks-to-me/), about splitting the Claude agent I talk to from the one coordinating work on my Stone Age remake. With [tmux-agents](https://github.com/TheClooneyCollection/tmux-agents), the new main agent has one peer: the coordinator, which handles the main Codex agent and its sub-agents. The post shows the setup before and after, why my conversation got quieter, and the extra hand-off that comes with it.
