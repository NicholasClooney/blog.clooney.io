I published [Start the Chain: My Three-Agent Setup Is Now One Command](/posts/start-the-chain/), a follow-up to the main agent post. The main agent, secondary and worker setup is now the agent-chain skill in [tmux-agents](https://github.com/TheClooneyCollection/tmux-agents), started with one command, and the post covers what changed with a week of real use and how projects talk to each other. It also covers what happens after a restart: `tmux-main-sequence` brings back a chain for every project, and `/resume` brings back the conversations that matter.
