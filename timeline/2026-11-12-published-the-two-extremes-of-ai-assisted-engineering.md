I published [The Two Extremes of AI-Assisted Engineering: Fast and Furious, vs. Slow and Careful](/posts/the-two-extremes-of-ai-assisted-engineering/), about the two opposite ways I work with the same coding agents: letting them run while I barely read the code, and slowing down until every decision is mine. It uses [EDControlRoom](https://github.com/TheClooneyCollection/EDControlRoom) as the example of a project moving along that spectrum, and argues that the real decision is where to set the quality bar for each project right now, and when to move it. This is the third version; I wrote it section by section after setting two earlier drafts aside.
