I published [How To Move Fast and NOT Break Things with AI](/posts/move-fast-and-not-break-things-with-ai/), about the point where using an agent's name as its id stopped fitting [tmux-agents](https://github.com/TheClooneyCollection/tmux-agents). It covers the move to real ids in progress for v1.9.0, and the tests, decision records and release rules that grew out of real use. The lesson is to prototype fast, then add structure where the problems show up and leave guard rails for the next agent.
