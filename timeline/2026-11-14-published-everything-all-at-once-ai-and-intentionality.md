I published [Everything All At Once: AI & Intentionality](/posts/everything-all-at-once-ai-and-intentionality/), which grew out of [an evening thinking entry](/timeline/2026-11-14-thinking-everything-at-once/) after a day of running an agent chain in every tmux window. With AI I can push four or five projects at once, but the bottleneck is still my own brain, and handing more decisions to agents means less of what I do is something I actually meant. The post is about noticing when that happens, and choosing, on some days, to slow down and stay with one or two things long enough to remember why each decision was made.
