I released [v1.9.0](https://github.com/TheClooneyCollection/skills/releases/tag/v1.9.0) of my [skills](https://github.com/TheClooneyCollection/skills). Every item in a `design-lab` gallery now has a short, stable ID in a chip with a Copy ID button, and each round's landing page lists every item with its ID. When the phone blocks the clipboard, the chip's text is selected and the page says "Selected, copy it". It came out of reviewing social preview cards from my phone, where pointing at "the third Glass card, the dark one" got old fast; now I paste `C3-glass-series-dark` back and the agents know exactly which one I mean.
